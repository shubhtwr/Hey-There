Why Hanging Out
With Me Is Actually
a Good Idea

Presented with evidence, Bollywood citations, and mild desperation.

Exhibit A

Your Mind Will Never Be Bored

"Talking a lot about unrelated things in general" is basically my middle name. Philosophy at 2 AM? Obviously. Debating whether Maggi counts as gourmet dining? Gladly. The one correct opinion on Dil Se's best song? We could go on for hours.

"The best conversations have no destination — they just keep going."

And if it rains? I become the affordable, slightly less chiseled version of SRK. We recreate Bollywood classics on demand. No budget required. Pure chaos guaranteed.

Exhibit B

The Tony & Pepper Dynamic

Minus the Iron Man suits. Minus the billion-dollar empire. Minus saving the universe from Thanos. Mostly just the banter — the quick, sharp, never-gets-old kind that makes you look forward to Mondays.

"I am Iron Man." — Not me. I cannot afford the suit. But the energy? Absolutely present.

Oxytocin is also involved. It's a neurochemical. You don't need to care about the science — just know it's good for you, and hanging out produces a lot of it.

Seven Reasons, in Good Faith

Each one is real. Some are scientifically adjacent. One involves a mild threat.

  • 01

    Unconditional Chai Commitment

    I will never, under any circumstances, suggest we skip tea. This is a non-negotiable value and a deeply held belief.

  • 02

    No Awkward Silences

    There is always something to say. Even when there isn't, I will find something. This is both a promise and a mild warning.

  • 03

    Encyclopedic Knowledge of Bad Movies

    IMDB under 5? I have seen it. I can quote it. I will subject you to it. You will hate it and then secretly quote it for months.

  • 04

    Delhi Genuinely Deserves to Be Explored

    Ghalib ka shahr hai. Urdu was born here. SRK was born here. We owe it to the city — and to ourselves — to actually experience it.

  • 05

    Comfortable Silence is a Superpower

    Star-counting is a completely valid plan. No agenda required. Some of the best moments have nothing happening in them.

  • 06

    Genuinely Good Company

    The kind where time moves differently and you check your phone less. That's a rarer thing than it sounds.

  • 07

    The Alternative is Worse

    If you're still not convinced, I find someone to do black magic on you. Either way, we're meeting. Might as well make it fun.

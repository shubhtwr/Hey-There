Six Things We Could
Actually, Genuinely Do

Sorted by vibe. Not by probability. All negotiable except the chai one. That one is non-negotiable.

Stupid Bollywood Movies

Let's binge everything with IMDB under 5. Achhi yaaden banaake kya hi karna hai — by the end, we'll either hate each other or become fluent in cringe. Both outcomes are acceptable.

Chaos Level: ★★★★★

Big Boss Marathon

Doosron ke ghar ke kalesh, but make it an event. Popcorn mandatory. Loud opinions even more mandatory. We will find something to fight about within 10 minutes. That is the fun part.

Drama Level: ★★★★★

Explore Delhi

Ghalib ka shahr. Urdu born here, SRK born here, culture layered like Mughal architecture. Kaise maaf kr paogi tum apne aapko if we don't at least find the gali wali chai?

Culture Level: ★★★★☆

Chai. Or Coffee. Or Both.

Pleaseeee. Let me be your cup of tea (pickup line, non-negotiable). We find the best cutting chai in Delhi. We debate life. We refill. We repeat. This one has no logical end point. That's the beauty of it.

Warmth Level: ★★★★★

Ek Baar, Dil Se

One viewing. I explain every scene. We discover if you fall in love with it the way I did. Fair warning: I will be emotionally invested. This is not casual movie-watching. Tissues strongly recommended.

Emotion Level: ★★★★★

Absolutely Nothing

We are allowed to do precisely nothing. Stare at the sky. Count stars until we lose count. Comfortable silence. No phones. No agenda. Just being somewhere, together. Sometimes that's enough.

Peace Level: ★★★★★
